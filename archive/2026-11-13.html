How the score works

Per 3-hour slot: 90 base + up to 10 for sun, minus rain and wind penalties. Rain of 0.1 mm or more caps it at 49; gusts of 40 km/h or more cap it at 49; gusts of 30–40 km/h keep it below 75. Good is 75 and up, fair 50–74. Temperature is shown, not scored.
